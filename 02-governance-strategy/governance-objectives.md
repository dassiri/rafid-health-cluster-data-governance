## 1. Purpose of this document

NDMO’s strategy specification requires **strategic goals and objectives**, plus strategic and operational performance metrics with targets across a 3-to-5-year strategy duration `[C]` (NDMO Standards v1.5, `DG.1.1`).

This document records **direction-level objectives** for Rafid `[A]`. It does **not** set metric targets. Targets, scorecards, and `DG.7` Data Governance KPIs are Phase 12.

---

## 2. Objective set

Objectives are numbered for traceability in later phases. Numbering is an internal document convention `[B]`, **not** an NDMO control ID.

### G-O1 — Establish authority for data `[A]` / `[B]`

Create a single, executive-sponsored source of authority for data decisions at cluster level, so data issues are not left only to IT or medical records.

**NDMO relevance:** Domain 1 defines governance as authority and control through people, process, and technology `[C]`. Role and committee implementation is `DG.4` (later) `[C]`.

### G-O2 — Adopt national guiding principles and an entity strategy spine `[A]` / `[C]`

Adopt NDMO Data Management Guiding Principles and complete the remaining strategy contents NDMO lists under `DG.1` over later phases (plan, approval, socialization).

**NDMO relevance:** `DG.1.1`–`DG.1.4` `[C]`. Completeness of `DG.1` is **not** claimed here.

### G-O3 — Bound what “enterprise” means `[A]` / `[B]`

Keep a stable governance scope: all cluster facilities, all recorded forms of data, and partners handling cluster/government data — with explicit exclusions for this documentation program.

**NDMO relevance:** Purpose and Scope of the Standards `[C]`. Rafid’s inclusion as a Public Entity remains `[NDMO verification required]` in real life; assumed in `[A]` for the case.

### G-O4 — Separate decision classes from execution `[B]`

Define which **classes** of decision are strategic, tactical, or operational before designing the operating model (Phase 3) and stewardship RACI (Phase 4).

**NDMO relevance:** NDMO names roles (`DG.4`) `[C]` but this objective does not assign a RACI. *Organizational Manual* duties `[NDMO verification required]`.

### G-O5 — Put policy in a hierarchy before writing policy `[B]` / `[C]`

Agree how Kingdom law, NDMO standards, sector rules, and entity documents stack, so Phase 6 does not produce orphan procedures.

**NDMO relevance:** `DG.2` requires entity policies and guidelines aligned to national policies and standards `[C]`. Gap analysis and policy text are not done here.

### G-O6 — Align by domain, do not claim compliance by slogan `[C]` / `[A]`

Use NDMO’s 15 domains and published control IDs as the alignment backbone; record evidence later; never invent controls.

**NDMO relevance:** 15-domain framework; 77 controls / 191 specifications; annual specification-level self-assessment described by NDMO `[C]`. This program has not performed that assessment.

### G-O7 — Prepare the cluster to govern high-risk health data `[A]` / `[B]`

Keep personal and clinical data visible as a first-class concern in later classification, quality, sharing, and protection work.

**NDMO relevance:** Domains 13, 14, 15 `[C]`. Domain 15 is under NCA mandate `[C]`. PDPL operational mapping `[NDMO verification required]`.
