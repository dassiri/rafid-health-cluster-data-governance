## 1. Purpose of this document

NDMO requires the entity’s Data Management and Personal Data Protection Strategy to include **Vision, Mission, and Strategic Goals and Objectives** `[C]` (NDMO Standards v1.5, specification `DG.1.1`).

This document records Rafid’s **working vision and mission** for the data governance program `[A]`. It is not a completed NDMO strategy (which `DG.1.1` also requires to cover challenges, internal and national requirements, 3-to-5-year metrics and targets, and initiative-level budget `[C]`). Those remaining `DG.1.1` elements are **not produced in Phase 1** and remain open.

Formal approval and socialization of the strategy (`DG.1.4`) are not performed in this portfolio drop `[C]` pointer only.

---

## 2. Vision `[A]`

**Vision:** Rafid Health Cluster uses trusted, protected, and fit-for-purpose data so that care is safer, operations are accountable, and data is shared only with a clear purpose and control.

This wording is a cluster assumption. It is directionally consistent with NDMO’s view of data as an asset that should be discoverable, protected, and held with clear accountability `[C]` (guiding principle **Data as a National Asset**), but it is **not** an NDMO-prescribed sentence.

---

## 3. Mission `[A]`

**Mission:** Establish and maintain enterprise authority, accountability, and repeatable practices for managing Rafid’s data — across all facilities and recorded forms — in alignment with NDMO Data Management and Personal Data Protection Standards and other applicable Kingdom requirements, without claiming compliance until evidence exists.

The mission states an **alignment intent** `[A]` + `[C]` (NDMO as the national standards source). It does not assert that Rafid has implemented the Standards.
