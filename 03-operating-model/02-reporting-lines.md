## 1. Purpose of this document

This document records **approved reporting lines** for the fictional Rafid Health Cluster `[A]`. Phase 1–2 left CDO placement unresolved. No CIO placement was treated as already approved.

Diagram: [`diagrams/org-reporting-lines.mmd`](diagrams/org-reporting-lines.mmd).

## 2. Options compared `[B]`

| Option | Strength | Weakness for Rafid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CIO / CDIO** | Near systems and budget; common in IT-led programmes | Makes governance look like an IT project. Owners disengage. Contradicts “IT is not accountable for business data.” CDO cannot credibly oversee CIO-as-custodian. |
| **CEO / executive office (approved)** | Authority across clinical and corporate data; matches Committee chair; supports CDO-led annual assessment as described by NDMO `[C]` | CEO span of control; DMO must stay thin. |
| **Cluster Medical Director** | Clinical credibility | Weak for finance/HR/revenue data; NDMO agenda is entity-wide, not clinical-only `[C]`. |
| **Quality / patient safety** | Safety culture | Quality does not own EMR platforms or finance data; tends to become indicator-governance only. |
| **CFO** | Control culture | Reads as cost, not care or national data agenda. |

## 3. Approved reporting lines

| Relationship | Type | Statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CDO → Cluster CEO | **Solid** | CDO reports **directly** to the Cluster CEO `[B]` |
| CIO / CDIO → Cluster CEO | **Solid** | CIO is a **peer** of the CDO, not the CDO’s manager `[B]` |
| Data Governance Officer → CDO | **Solid** | DGO sits in the Data Management Office `[B]` / NDMO names both roles `[C]` |
| PDPO → Cluster CEO | **Solid** | PDPO reports to the CEO `[B]` |
| PDPO → CDO | **Dotted** | Working relationship only `[B]` |
| Compliance Officer (data) → Legal / Internal Control | **Solid** | CO is not the CDO marking their own homework `[B]` |
| Compliance Officer (data) → CDO | **Dotted** | Coordination on the data agenda `[B]` |
| CDO → CIO | **None** | The CDO–CIO dotted “tooling” line was **rejected** and is not used |

**Not used:** CDO solid or dotted to CIO; PDPO solely under CIO; Compliance Officer solid to CDO.

---

## 4. Why this placement `[B]`

- The CDO leads the entity data management and personal data protection **agenda** as named by NDMO `[C]`. NDMO also describes the CDO as leading the annual compliance exercise `[C]`. That work spans clinical and corporate data, so the CDO is not nested under the CIO.
- The CIO remains the executive for systems and Data Custodians, and sits on the Data Management Committee as a **peer**.
- PDPO is not buried in IT. PDPL DPO equivalence remains `[NDMO verification required]`.
- Compliance Officer dotted to CDO and solid to Legal/Internal Control preserves a control challenge. Relationship to Internal Audit is `[NDMO verification required]`.

## 5. Data Management Committee (not a line report)

The Data Management Committee is a **forum**, not a box on the org chart. The Cluster CEO **chairs** it. The CDO is a member and does **not** chair their own oversight `[B]`. See [`06-governance-forums.md`](06-governance-forums.md).
