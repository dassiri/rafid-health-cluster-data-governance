## 1. Purpose of this document

This document maps Rafid **working titles** `[B]` to NDMO **role and structure names** `[C]` from the *Data Management and Personal Data Protection Standards*, Version 1.5, January 2021.

It does **not**:

- Claim NDMO compliance
- Treat specification-level control IDs as verified
- Reproduce or invent control numbers
- Replace the NDMO *Organizational Manual* (not retrieved) `[NDMO verification required]`

Phase 2 recorded some specification-level identifiers. **Phase 3 does not re-assert those identifiers as verified controls.** Any control-level NDMO reference in this programme remains `[NDMO verification required]`.

## 3. What `[C]` covers here vs what it does not

**`[C]` in this file means:** the **name** appears in NDMO Standards v1.5 (role list, structure names, CDO-led annual assessment description, Domain 15 under NCA, Business Data Executive mentioned in the sharing process as stated in the Standards, senior-executive strategy approval).

**`[C]` does not mean:** Rafid has implemented the control, appointed the officer, aligned job descriptions to the Organizational Manual, or passed an assessment.

**Control-level IDs:** not cited as verified in Phase 3. `[NDMO verification required]`.
