## 1. Purpose

Appoint or change a Data Owner and Business Data Steward so each domain has **exactly one** registered Owner `[B]`.

## 2. Process

```text
Nomination → Review → DMC ratification → Registry update
```

| Step | Who | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nomination** | Current Owner, CDO, or CEO (for initial/vacant seats) | Propose Owner **title** and Steward **title** for one domain; confirm no second **A** |
| **Review** | DMO | Completeness: domain exists (Phase 4 list) or new-domain paper; no IT-as-Owner; DMO not proposed as business Owner |
| **DMC ratification** | DMC **A** | Approve or deny Owner (and note Steward). CEO chairs `[B]` |
| **Registry update** | DMO | Ownership Registry, Steward Registry, Ownership Change Record |

## 3. Roles

| Role | RACI |
| --- | --- |
| DMC | **A** for Owner ratification |
| CDO / DMO | **R** for pack and registries |
| Proposed Owner | **C** (must concur) |
| Custodian | **I** |
| PDPO / CO | **I** unless the change affects privacy operating seats |

## 4. Inputs / outputs / evidence

| Inputs | Outputs | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nomination paper; current registry extract | DMC decision | Ownership Change Record; updated registries |

## 5. Escalation

If DMC cannot agree: residual → Cluster CEO (Phase 3). Unassigned domain: Unassigned-Domain Escalation (STD-001). No numeric SLA.

Year-1 eight assignments in STD-001 are the **approved design map**; live minutes are not claimed `[A]`.
