## 2. Quality responsibilities (locked)

| Role | Quality responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Data Owner** | **Accountable** for business quality outcomes in the domain: CDE confirmation, quality rules, thresholds for those rules, issue closure, residual risk. |
| **Business Data Steward** | **Responsible** for operational quality management: candidates, registry content, monitoring, logging, RCA proposal, verification. |
| **IT Data Steward / Custodian** | **Technical remediation** and measurement enablement. Never **A** for business quality meaning. |
| **DMO** | **Framework ownership** and **registry oversight**. Coordinates; not business Owner of a domain (including Domain 8: DMO hosts the Steward only). |
| **DMC** | **Governance escalation and decisions** (policy-level, Owner non-participation, out-of-policy exceptions). CEO chairs. |
| **PDPO** | **Consulted** where quality issues involve personal-data / privacy implications. Not Owner of the quality defect. |
