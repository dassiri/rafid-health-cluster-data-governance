## 1. Purpose

This document applies **Phase 4 ownership exactly** to Core MDM entities.

It does **not** modify the Phase 4 RACI. Pointer: `04-ownership-stewardship/03-ownership-decision-matrix.md`

It does **not** introduce new Data Owner titles.

---

## 2. Approved Data Owner titles (locked) `[A]`

| Master | Data Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Patient / Person Master | Patient Access & Experience Director |
| Provider / Clinician Master | Medical Affairs Officer |
| Facility / Organization Master | Strategy & Planning Director |

**Steward** in this phase’s examples: **Business Data Steward** only (no named operational titles).

IT / Custodian is never business Owner of these masters.

DMO is not Patient or Provider Owner. For Facility / Organization, DMO may **host** the Domain 8 Steward (Phase 4); DMO is still **not** Owner.
