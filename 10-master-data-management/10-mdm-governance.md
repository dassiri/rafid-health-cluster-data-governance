## 2. Responsibilities (locked)

| Role | MDM governance |
| --- | --- |
| **MDM governance (programme)** | CDO / DMO own **methodology**, registers, and cross-domain coordination — not Person meaning |
| **Domain Owner (Data Owner)** | **A** for master **business definition**, domain rules, match/merge **intent**, survivorship **rules for that domain**, residual duplicate risk |
| **Business Data Steward** | **R** for duplicate review, registry content, operational matching queue, quality monitoring |
| **DMO** | Methodology, Master Domain/Entity registries, clash routing, lineage/catalog consistency checks |
| **DMC** | Governance escalation and decisions within the approved Phase 3 governance authority and decision-right framework |
| **IT / Custodian** | Technical implementation in existing systems; never **A** for master meaning |

---

## 3. DMC boundary (locked wording)

> Governance escalation and decisions within the approved Phase 3 governance authority and decision-right framework.

**DMC is not the routine approver of every master entity.**

No new DMC decision right. Routine approve of definition/domain rules is **Data Owner** ([`13-master-data-lifecycle.md`](13-master-data-lifecycle.md)). Policy exceptions remain **DMC = A** via the existing exception procedure.
