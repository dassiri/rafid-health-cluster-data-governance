## 2. Responsibilities (locked)

| Role | Lifecycle responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Data Owner** | **Accountable** for lifecycle decisions for the data domain (including approving retention **rules** once a verified period exists) |
| **Business Data Steward** | Maintains lifecycle **metadata** and supports operational execution |
| **DMO** | Lifecycle governance **methodology**; **registry / oversight**; governance monitoring |
| **IT / Custodian** | Implements **approved** technical retention, archive, and disposal mechanisms — does not decide the business rule |
| **PDPO** | Privacy compliance **input** where personal data is involved — not Owner of the domain lifecycle |
| **DMC** | Governance escalation and decisions within the approved Phase 3 governance authority and decision-right framework |

**No new DMC decision right.** DMC is not the routine approver of every retention rule.
