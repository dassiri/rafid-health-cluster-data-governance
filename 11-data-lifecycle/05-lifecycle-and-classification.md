---

## 3. Critical statement (locked)

**Classification does NOT automatically determine retention duration.**

Retention must consider:

- Business need  
- Legal / regulatory requirements  
- Records requirements  
- Contractual obligations where applicable  
- Litigation / legal hold  
- Privacy considerations  

Specific retention requirements remain `[NDMO verification required]`.  
Healthcare-specific retention remains `[Legal / regulatory verification required]`.

A Public dataset is not “dispose immediately.” A Restricted dataset is not “retain forever.” Both still need an Owner-approved **rule** after verification.
