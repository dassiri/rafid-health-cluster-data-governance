## 2. Principles (locked) `[A][B]`

| Principle | Intent |
| --- | --- |
| **Outcome-focused** | Measure whether governance **outcomes** exist (assigned Owner, classified dataset, closed issue, evidenced disposal) — not slide volume or meeting attendance as success |
| **Evidence-based** | Every KPI names an evidence source. No evidence → no claimed result |
| **Proportionate** | Year-1 measures what Phase 4–11 already designed. Do not invent a second quality, catalog, or MDM framework to feed a metric |
| **Owner-accountable** | Data Owner **A** for domain interpretation of domain KPIs (Phase 4). DMO owns methodology and the catalogue |
| **No vanity metrics** | Do not treat “number of policies written,” “catalog page hits,” or “meetings held” as proof of control |
| **No invented live results** | No KPI value in this repository is real Rafid performance |
| **Baseline before target** | Where no baseline exists: **To be established after baseline measurement.** |
| **Inform, do not auto-score maturity** | **KPIs inform maturity; they do not automatically determine maturity** |
| **IDs are Rafid, not NDMO** | `DG-KPI-00n` is `[A]`. It is **not** `DG.7` or any NDMO specification ID |
| **Reuse forums** | Report through existing Phase 3 cadence. Do not invent a KPI committee |

DMC: Governance escalation and decisions within the approved Phase 3 governance authority and decision-right framework. **No new DMC decision right.**
