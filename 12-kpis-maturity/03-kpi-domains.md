## 1. Purpose

This document records **twelve KPI domains** `[A]` mapped to existing Phases 3–11.

These are **measurement slices**. They are **not** a ninth Data Owner, not a new RACI, and not NDMO knowledge-domain IDs.

Steward references use **Business Data Steward** only.

| Supply Chain & Asset Data | Supply Chain Director |
