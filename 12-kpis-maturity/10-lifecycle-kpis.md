## 2. KPIs in this domain

| KPI ID | Role |
| --- | --- |
| DG-KPI-009 | Executive — Retention Schedule Coverage (rule **registered**, not period verified) |
| DG-KPI-017 | Supporting — Disposal Evidence Completeness (closed disposals with Disposal Record fields occupied) |

DG-KPI-009 **must not** be briefed as “% legally compliant retention.” Placeholder periods remain `[NDMO verification required]` / `[Legal / regulatory verification required]`. Coverage of a rule with a placeholder is **governance occupancy**, not legal compliance.

Legal/regulatory hold remains an **override** of disposal (Phase 11). Hold volume is not an executive KPI in the locked 12; it may be a DMC qualitative note.
