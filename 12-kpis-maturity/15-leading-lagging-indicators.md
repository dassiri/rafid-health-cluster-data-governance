## 1. Purpose

This document classifies catalogue KPIs as **leading** or **lagging** `[A][B]`.

Labels are design choices. They are not NDMO-mandated `[NDMO verification required]`.

---

## 2. Definitions `[B]`

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Leading** | Indicates whether conditions for control **exist** (registers occupied, reviews done, rules recorded) before an incident is counted |
| **Lagging** | Describes an **outcome or stock** that has already occurred (closure rate, open count, assignment state) |

Some KPIs have **both** uses (for example Critical Issue Aging is a stock that **leads** further harm if ignored).
