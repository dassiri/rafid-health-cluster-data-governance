## 1. Purpose

This document records **who sees which KPI, using existing Phase 3 structure**.

```text
Executive / DMC
→ Governance Management (CDO / DMO)
→ Domain Owners / Stewards
→ Operational Teams
```

## 2. Forum use (Phase 3 frequencies)

| Audience | Phase 3 forum | Frequency | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Executive / DMC** | Data Management Committee | Quarterly + extraordinary | Executive 12-KPI pack; exception themes; Critical aging themes. Chair remains Cluster CEO. CDO does **not** chair |
| **Governance management** | DMO operations huddle | Weekly | Intake, routing, Critical list — **no** policy |
| **Cross-domain** | Steward Forum | Monthly | Full operational pack including supporting KPIs; clash detection |
| **Domain** | Domain huddles | Biweekly or monthly by domain risk | Domain slice of quality, catalog, issues; Owner **A** |
| **Operational teams** | Custodian / process teams (not a fifth board) | As directed by Owner/Steward | Remediation work |

DMC: Governance escalation and decisions within the approved Phase 3 governance authority and decision-right framework. **No new DMC decision right.**

Phase 3 already lists **KPI pack** as a DMC input. This phase fills **what** that pack contains. It does not add a DMC class.
