## 3. Forbidden automations

| Forbidden | Why |
| --- | --- |
| “DG-KPI-001 = 100% ⇒ Ownership Level 5” | Assignment of titles ≠ optimized operation |
| “Average of 12 KPIs = maturity level” | Mixes unlike units; vanity |
| “DG-KPI-009 high ⇒ legally compliant lifecycle” | Coverage ≠ verified period |
