## 1. How do you measure data governance?

Outcome-focused KPIs with evidence, reported through **existing Phase 3 forums** `[A][B]`. Hierarchy: Strategic Objective → Governance Outcome → KPI → Metric Definition → Evidence Source → Reporting → Decision/Action. I documented **12 executive KPIs** (`DG-KPI-001`–`012`) as **fictional Rafid IDs `[A]`**, not NDMO control IDs. No value is real Rafid performance. Targets: **To be established after baseline measurement.**

---

## 2. What is the difference between a KPI and a metric?

A **metric** is the defined calculation. A **KPI** is the named indicator used for governance **decision/action** `[B]`. Vanity counts without a decision path are not executive KPIs.

---

## 3. Why only twelve executive KPIs?

10–15 is the bound `[A][B]`. Twelve cover ownership, stewardship, classification, metadata, quality (without an enterprise index), issues, lineage, MDM duplicates, retention-rule occupancy, sharing privacy review, exceptions, and Critical aging. More belong in the supporting catalogue, not the DMC cover sheet.

---

**No.** `DG-KPI-001` is a Rafid identifier `[A]`. NDMO `DG.7` is a control **name** already recorded in Phase 2 `[C]`. Phase 12 does **not** claim `DG.7` is implemented. I do not invent specification IDs.

Five levels: Initial, Developing, Defined, Managed, Optimized `[A][B]`. Ten areas aligned to Phases 3–11 plus measurement. ## 8. Do KPIs determine maturity?

**KPIs inform maturity; they do not automatically determine maturity.**

## 9. Who sees the dashboard?

Conceptual views only — no software. DMC quarterly; CDO/DMO management; Owner + **Business Data Steward** domain; operational lists. Cadence is Phase 3: DMC quarterly, Steward Forum monthly, domain huddles biweekly/monthly, DMO huddle weekly.

## 10. Who approves a new KPI?

Defined and validated by DMO; CDO for catalogue/method; Data Owner **A** for domain interpretation; Policy conflicts use the existing exception procedure. **No new DMC decision right.** DMC: Governance escalation and decisions within the approved Phase 3 governance authority and decision-right framework.

---

## 11. How would you start measurement in healthcare?

Use the 12 executive KPIs on **existing registers** (Ownership, Classification, catalog, scorecards, lineage, MDM, Retention Schedule, sharing, exceptions). Do not invent retention clocks. Do not claim NDMO/legal compliance. MVP quality/catalog/lineage/MDM/lifecycle scopes stay as Phases 7–11.
