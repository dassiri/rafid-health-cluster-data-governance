## 2. Five levels (locked — reused)

| Level | Name | Meaning `[A][B]` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Initial** | Ad hoc. Roles, registers, or processes are missing or heroic |
| **2** | **Developing** | Intent exists; fragments documented; occupancy incomplete |
| **3** | **Defined** | Framework documented and understood; processes described; roles titled |
| **4** | **Managed** | Processes operated with evidence; KPIs evidenced; exceptions controlled |
| **5** | **Optimized** | Evidence-based improvement is routine; measures drive action without vanity |

This documentation programme, by itself, does **not** place Rafid at Level 5. Documentation without operation is at most a **Defined** *design* — and even that is **not** recorded as a scored Rafid result.

NDMO’s binary specification-level assessment remains Phase 14 and is **not** this model `[C]` as already distinguished in Phase 12.

## 4. Characteristics by level `[A][B]`

These are **conceptual** characteristics. They are not a scored Rafid profile.

| Dimension | Level 1 Initial | Level 2 Developing | Level 3 Defined | Level 4 Managed | Level 5 Optimized |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Governance** | Ad hoc forums; decisions undocumented | Fragmentary RACI; irregular packs | Operating model, forums, and decision-right classes documented | Forums operate with records; exceptions controlled under existing **A**s | Improvement actions close routinely; cadence used without vanity attendance metrics |
| **Ownership** | Unclear or heroic ownership | Partial titles; Steward informal | Eight Owner titles and Business Data Steward type documented | Registries occupied; vacant Owner escalated on the existing path | Ownership changes are governed; participation evidenced |
| **Data Quality** | Issues handled case-by-case | Some rules or CDEs listed | Dimensions, 13 illustrative CDEs, rules, and scorecard **structure** defined | Dimension results evidenced; issues close under the existing procedure; composite does not mask breach | Quality measures drive remediation and design change |
| **Metadata** | Assets undescribed | Partial catalog fragments | Minimum metadata, glossary, and catalog MVP defined | Published assets meet mandatory occupancy; review cycle evidenced | Metadata completeness and certification inform other controls without vanity search-hit KPIs |
| **Lineage** | Unknown origin/use | Informal diagrams | Criticality method and Critical Lineage Register defined | Confirmed Critical flows registered, complete, and reviewed on cycle | Lineage used for change/disposal impact as routine practice |
| **Classification** | Unlabeled operational data | Inconsistent local labels | Four Rafid tiers and process defined | In-scope datasets classified by Owner; unlabeled not treated as Public | Classification used consistently in handling without Restricted-volume vanity |
| **MDM** | Duplicate identity unmanaged | Local matching folklore | Logical golden record, Core vs supporting, duplicate review defined | Duplicate cases resolved with occupancy; no match-score theatre | Identity quality improves through governed review, not engine vanity |
| **Lifecycle** | Keep forever by silence | Informal archive/delete | Create→Dispose model and Retention Schedule **structure** defined | Retention rules registered; disposal records complete; holds override dispose | Lifecycle occupancy and disposal evidence inform risk without invented legal clocks |
| **Measurement / KPIs** | No measures, or vanity counts | Scattered metrics without owners | KPI catalogue, formulas, owners, and dashboard spec defined | KPIs evidenced, quality-checked, and reviewed in existing forums | Measures refresh under change control; they **inform** maturity and do not auto-set it |

**KPIs inform maturity; they do not automatically determine maturity.**

## 5. What a high KPI does not prove

| Forbidden shortcut | Why |
| --- | --- |
| DG-KPI-001 = 100% ⇒ Ownership Level 5 | Title assignment ≠ optimized operation |
| Average of 12 KPIs = maturity level | Mixes unlike units; vanity |
| DG-KPI-009 high ⇒ legally compliant lifecycle | Coverage ≠ verified period |
| Phase 8 inventory exists ⇒ Metadata Level 4 | Teaching inventory ≠ operated catalog process |
