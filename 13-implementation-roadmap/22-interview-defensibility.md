## 1. How would you implement Data Governance from scratch?

Design first (strategy, operating model, ownership, policy), then **mobilize forums**, then **activate Owners and Business Data Stewards**, then pilot **locked MVPs**, then **scale on evidence**. Governance before tooling. Designed ≠ Operational ≠ Measured.

---

## 2. What would you do in the first 90 days?

**`[A]` Illustrative:** WS1 (DMC rhythm, DMO intake) and start WS2/WS11/WS12. Do not buy a catalog. Do not invent retention years. No real 90-day calendar is claimed for Rafid.

---

## 3. What would you prioritize in Year 1?

Establish & Pilot: Owner/Steward activation, policy adoption, classification, quality/catalog/lineage/MDM/lifecycle **MVPs as already locked**, KPI baseline, NDMO evidence **preparation** (not compliance).

---

## 4. Why not implement a catalog tool immediately?

Mandatory catalog fields need Owner, Steward, and classification (WS2+WS4 before WS6). A tool without occupancy is vanity. **No platform is named** in this roadmap.

---

## 5. How do you sequence Data Quality, Metadata, Lineage and MDM?

Quality/lineage/MDM **benefit** from catalog but can **start narrowly**. Catalog itself waits on Owner + classification. Not strictly linear.

---

## 6. How do you get Data Owners engaged?

One title per domain (Phase 4). CEO-chaired DMC. Vacant Owner is an existing escalation. Onboarding covers **A** decisions, not theatre.

---

## 7. How do you handle resistance?

Change chain: awareness → onboarding → pilot → adoption → measurement → reinforcement. Keep forums to four Phase 3 cadences. Risk #4.

---

## 8. How do you prioritize domains?

Qualitative: impact, safety, risk, dependency, complexity, readiness, value. **`[A]` High impact + reasonable readiness → early rollout.** **`[A]` High impact + low readiness → foundation, not skip.** No real score.

---

## 9. How do you measure roadmap success?

Phase 12 KPIs **inform**. Gates use evidence occupancy. “Not evidenced” is allowed. **No real KPI results** in this repo.

---

## 10. What happens if the roadmap falls behind?

Do not open M13. Catch-up in MVP scope. Change control under **existing** authority. Competing priorities = risk #12.

---

## 11. How do KPIs influence the roadmap?

**KPIs inform roadmap decisions. They do NOT automatically determine maturity. They do NOT automatically determine roadmap priority.**

---

## 12. How do you align with NDMO?

Use Phase 2 method and verification gaps. Do not invent control IDs. This roadmap is **not** `DG.1.3`. **Not compliance.**

## 13. How would you explain the three-year roadmap to executives?

Year 1 establish and pilot locked MVPs. Year 2 scale **only if** Year-1 evidence exists. Year 3 institutionalize — still no named tech platform. **`[A]` Illustrative.** Not a real Rafid plan. Not NDMO-required sequencing.
