## 1. Purpose

This document defines the three evidence states used throughout Phase 14.

**This distinction must be repeated clearly.**

NDMO’s own annual assessment (specification-level, binary if partial) `[C]` is a **different** scoring model. Rafid has **not** applied it. These three states are a **portfolio evidence model** `[A][B]`.

---

## 2. DESIGNED

**Meaning:** Documented governance design exists.

Examples: POL-001 text in the repository; Phase 4 title map; conceptual catalog; KPI definitions with no values.

---

## 3. OPERATIONAL

**Meaning:** The capability is actually operating with real organizational roles, processes, records, and evidence.

Examples (not claimed): signed appointments, populated Ownership Registry, DMC minutes from a real meeting, classified live datasets.

---

## 4. MEASURED

**Meaning:** Operational evidence exists and performance has been measured over time.

Examples (not claimed): evidenced DG-KPI results after baseline; trend of issue closure; a completed maturity assessment with real occupancy.
