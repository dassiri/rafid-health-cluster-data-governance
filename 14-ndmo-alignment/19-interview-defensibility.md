## 1. Is this project NDMO compliant?

**No.** It is a portfolio governance framework designed around NDMO-alignment principles. I deliberately separated verified NDMO information from fictional organizational design and left control-level requirements marked for verification rather than inventing them.

The Rafid framework is a portfolio design exercise. Its artifacts demonstrate how a Data Governance function could be structured; they are not evidence that Rafid Health Cluster has operationally implemented or achieved NDMO compliance.

---

## 2. How did you avoid hallucinating NDMO controls?

I used source labels and a verification register. Where exact control/specification text was unavailable, I marked it `[NDMO verification required]` rather than creating plausible control IDs.

I reused only IDs already recorded from Standards v1.5 in Phase 2 (`DG.1`–`DG.8`, selected `DG` specifications, `MCM`, `DC`, `DC.3.2` / `DC.3.3`). I did not invent `MCM.1` or other uncopied IDs.

---

## 3. What is the difference between alignment and compliance?

Alignment means the framework is structured to address relevant governance areas. Compliance requires verified requirements, approved implementation, operational evidence, and assessment.

---

## 4. What evidence would you need in a real organization?

Approved policies, role appointments, ownership records, classification decisions, quality issue records, catalog records, lineage evidence, MDM records, lifecycle records, KPI evidence, and governance decision records.

Those would be **Operational** (and later **Measured**) evidence. This repository is **Designed** only.
